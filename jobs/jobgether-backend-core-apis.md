### Full description (verbatim)

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Backend Engineer, Core APIs based in Brazil.

Join a fully remote engineering team building technology that helps leading organizations detect and prevent online fraud.

In this role, you’ll design and scale high-performance backend systems responsible for real-time data processing and critical API services.

You’ll work across Go and Node.js to build reliable, scalable infrastructure that supports rapidly growing usage.

The role offers meaningful ownership, from investigating complex technical problems to designing, implementing, testing, and deploying solutions.

You’ll collaborate with globally distributed engineers and cross-functional teams in a highly asynchronous environment.

Your work will directly contribute to the performance, reliability, and scalability of fraud detection capabilities used by major technology companies.

Accountabilities

Design, develop, and optimize scalable backend systems supporting real-time data processing and high-performance web services.
Build, maintain, and improve APIs, microservices, and backend infrastructure with a strong focus on reliability, scalability, and performance.
Develop primarily with Go and/or Node.js while collaborating across services and technologies to deliver robust backend solutions.
Partner with cross-functional engineering teams to integrate backend components and ensure systems operate efficiently at scale.
Investigate complex or problematic features through technical deep dives, root-cause analysis, hypothesis development, experimentation, and data-driven evaluation.
Conduct performance tuning, debugging, testing, and reliability improvements across backend systems.
Contribute to a data-driven engineering culture by sharing technical knowledge, best practices, and learnings with colleagues.
Take ownership of engineering work from initial concept and investigation through implementation, deployment, and iteration.
Participate in a shared on-call rotation, with schedules communicated in advance and coverage balanced across the team.
Mentor junior developers and support the technical and professional growth of teammates.

Requirements

2+ years of professional backend software development experience, with demonstrated experience building production-grade systems.
Strong hands-on experience with Go and/or Node.js, with a willingness to deepen expertise across both technologies.
Experience designing, developing, and maintaining scalable backend systems, APIs, real-time data-processing services, and microservice architectures.
Strong understanding of SQL and experience working with databases or data stores such as DynamoDB, Redis, or Elasticsearch.
Solid knowledge of software engineering fundamentals and development tools, including Git, IDEs, shell scripting, and CI/CD workflows.
Ability to investigate complex technical problems independently, analyze system behavior, form hypotheses, run experiments, and turn findings into practical improvements.
Strong communication skills in English, with the ability to collaborate effectively across a globally distributed and asynchronous team.
Ability to work independently while contributing constructively to team decisions, technical discussions, and shared engineering goals.
Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation is a plus.
Additional experience with TypeScript, Express, ClickHouse, dbt, Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or Databricks is beneficial.
Familiarity with observability and monitoring platforms such as Datadog, as well as modern logging and telemetry practices, is preferred.
Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, and modern containerization and orchestration technologies is an advantage.
Experience with both Go and Node.js in a backend context, as well as knowledge of Internet security and privacy mechanisms, is highly valued.

Benefits

Fully remote work environment with the ability to work from an authorized home location.
International, distributed team with an asynchronous and collaborative working culture.
Candidates must be authorized to work from their home location; visa sponsorship is not provided.
